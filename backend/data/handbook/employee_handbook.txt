ACME Corp – Employee Handbook  (v3.2, effective Jan 2025)
==========================================================

SECTION 1 – LEAVE POLICY
--------------------------
Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per completed month of service.
Unused leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.
To request leave, submit a request via the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance.
Emergency leave requests should be communicated to your direct manager immediately by phone.

Sick Leave
All employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.
Sick leave does not accrue; unused sick leave does not carry over.

Maternity & Paternity Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid maternity / adoption leave.
Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid paternity leave.
Both must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or placement.

SECTION 2 – CODE OF CONDUCT
-----------------------------
ACME Corp expects all employees to act with integrity, respect, and professionalism.
Discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind is strictly prohibited.
Employees who witness or experience misconduct should report it to HR or use the
anonymous whistleblower hotline: 1-800-ACME-ETH.

Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any personal financial interest in a supplier, customer, or competitor.
Outside employment that conflicts with ACME's business interests requires prior written approval from HR.

SECTION 3 – REMOTE WORK POLICY
--------------------------------
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Remote workers must be reachable during core hours: 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM local time.
A secure VPN connection is mandatory when accessing company systems from outside the office.
The company will reimburse up to $500 per year for home-office equipment.

SECTION 4 – BENEFITS
----------------------
Health Insurance
ACME covers 100 % of the premium for the employee and 70 % for dependants on the standard plan.
Open enrolment occurs each November for coverage starting the following January.

Retirement
The company matches 401(k) contributions up to 4 % of base salary.
Vesting is immediate for employee contributions; employer match vests over 3 years (cliff).

Learning & Development
Each employee receives an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, conferences, or books.
Expenses must be pre-approved by the direct manager and submitted within 30 days of completion.

SECTION 5 – PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
---------------------------------
Formal performance reviews are held twice a year: in June and December.
Ratings scale: Exceeds Expectations / Meets Expectations / Needs Improvement.
Employees who receive two consecutive "Needs Improvement" ratings will be placed on a
Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) with a 90-day remediation window.

SECTION 6 – IT & DATA SECURITY
--------------------------------
All company laptops must use full-disk encryption and run the approved endpoint-protection suite.
Employees must not store customer data on personal devices or unapproved cloud services.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and changed every 90 days.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all corporate accounts.
Any suspected security incident must be reported to security@acme.com within 1 hour of discovery.

SECTION 7 – EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
-----------------------------------
Business travel expenses must be submitted via Concur within 30 days of the trip.
Meals are reimbursed at a maximum of $75 per day for domestic travel and $100 for international.
Receipts are required for any single expense over $25.
First-class flights require VP-level approval.
